Wire probe wizard
Use when the connection check fails. It changes GPIO modes and may switch the scooter off. It cannot prove an unknown wire is safe.


  1. Measure voltages first. Leave out red, supply and battery-voltage wires.
  2. Connect ESP32 GND to verified ground, then unknown safe wires to your chosen GPIOs.
  3. Enter 1–6 distinct GPIO numbers and run the probe.


Allowed: 4, 5, 13, 14, 16–19, 21–23, 25–27, 32, 33.



Data/status frames → ESC TX → ESP32 RX. Held high → possible ESC RX, but could be a supply: verify first. Held low → possible ground or driven-low line. Floating → RX or keep-alive; if the scooter switches off, investigate keep-alive. Activity without Brightway frames is inconclusive, especially for LEQI.


After probing, restore the normal wiring above and run Check connection again. Keep-alive is restored high when the probe ends.
